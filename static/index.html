01 / THE SERIES

A CHAMPIONSHIP BUILT ON COMPETITION.

AGP is a proposed multi-round, sprint-distance championship modeled around the intensity of club racing: America’s top domestic athletes, professional teams and emerging talent competing in men’s and women’s classifications on accessible city courses. Every element shown here is illustrative.
